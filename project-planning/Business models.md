
### Creators Pay For Usage

Creators can enable their apps to use Unstoppable cloud services and they pay for usage. 

### Users Subscriptions

This model would allow creators to build apps at 0$ cost, enabling their users to connect a subscription that would allow to use Unstoppable cloud services to backup data, run remote better inference models or other capabilities. 

App builders could get rewarded by the usage of the subscription through their Apps.
