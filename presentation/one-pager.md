# The Unstoppable Stack — One-Pager

*For quick sharing before or after the presentation.*

---

## What

A toolchain that enables developers to **vibe-code apps** using Holepunch (P2P),
QVAC (local AI), and WDK (crypto payments) — with any AI coding assistant.

## The Problem

AI-assisted development ("vibe-coding") is the future of software. AI is already
**excellent** at building centralized apps — React, Next.js, AWS, Stripe — in
minutes. Centralized development has never been easier.

But AI can't build with the Holepunch/Tether stack. It doesn't know the APIs,
puts P2P modules in the wrong process, and every project starts with hours of
debugging. **If AI can't build with our stack, developers won't choose it** —
no matter how good the technology is. Every day AI gets better at centralized
development, the gap widens. This is an existential risk for adoption.

## The Flip Side

If we solve the AI knowledge problem, our stack has a **structural advantage**:
developers don't need servers, don't pay for hosting, don't manage infrastructure.
They just build the app. With centralized, you vibe-code in minutes then need
AWS + DevOps. With the Unstoppable Stack, you vibe-code and you're done.

## How

**1. CLI** — `npx create-unstoppable-app`
Scaffolds a project pre-configured for your AI assistant of choice.

**2. Agent Pack** — Battle-tested knowledge for AI assistants
12 module docs, 10 skills, 17 recipes, reusable components, pinned versions,
and troubleshooting guides. Works with Claude Code, Cursor, and OpenCode.

**3. Website** — unstoppable.dev
Documentation, getting started guides, and a developer community hub.

## Proof

We built the same P2P desktop app (Coffee Assistant) twice:
- **Without** the agent pack: 50+ prompts, 6 critical bugs, hours of debugging
- **With** the agent pack: 2 prompts, 0 bugs, minutes

## The Vision — A Platform

The toolchain is Phase 1. The bigger vision:

**Phase 2 — Unstoppable Auth**
Local-first identity. One keypair across all unstoppable apps.
No passwords, no auth servers. Device linking and recovery built in.

**Phase 3 — Unstoppable Subscriptions**
Cloud services for P2P apps — without the developer needing servers.

| Free (local) | Subscriber |
|-------------|-----------|
| On-device AI (3B models) | Cloud inference (70B+) |
| Local storage | Cloud-backed persistence |
| Files online when device is on | Always-available file seeding |

- Users subscribe via USDt (WDK)
- Tether provides infrastructure (inference, storage, seeding)
- **Revenue is shared with app builders**
- Builders never touch a server. They just build the app.

## The Flywheel

Better tooling → more developers → more apps → more users → more
subscribers → revenue → fund better tooling. Every payment in USDt.
