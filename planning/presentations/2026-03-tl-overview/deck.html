The Unstoppable Stack

Toolchain to scaffold and vibe-code P2P apps with local AI. Holepunch + Tether stack.

1. SCAFFOLD
us init & us scaffold
Pick platform (Pear Desktop, Expo Mobile, Node CLI), features (P2P, KV, AI, multi-writer), and AI assistant.
2. VIBE-CODE
Agent Pack
Rules & recipes injected into Claude, Cursor, OpenCode, or Codex. The AI knows P2P patterns, QVAC, the full stack.
3. RUN
Unstoppable Node
Personal sync hub. Replicates data, seeds files, delegates AI. Apps pair automatically over Hyperswarm.
